Physics & Mathematical Theory Guide

1. Hooke's Law & Restoration Dynamics

Robert Hooke (1678) established that within the elastic limit, the restoring force exerted by a spring is directly proportional to displacement from its rest position:

F_s = -k · Δx = -k · (y - L₀)

Where $k$ is the spring stiffness constant (N/m) and $\Delta x$ is total extension. At static equilibrium in a gravitational field $g$:

Δx_eq = (m · g) / k

2. Equation of Motion & Damped Oscillation

Summing forces under gravity, viscous dashpot damping ($F_d = -c \cdot v$), and external driving force:

m · (d²x/dt²) + c · (dx/dt) + k · x = F₀ · cos(ω t)

The natural undamped angular frequency $\omega_0$ and period $T$ are given by:

ω₀ = √(k / m),   T = 2π √(m / k),   c_crit = 2 √(k · m)

3. Spring Combinations

Parallel Springs: k_eq = k₁ + k₂
Stiffness adds together.
Series Springs: 1/k_eq = 1/k₁ + 1/k₂
Compliance adds together.

4. Elastic Yield & Plasticity

When tensile stress crosses the material's yield strength ($\sigma_y$), dislocations migrate through the atomic lattice, producing irreversible elongation ($\Delta L_p$). Beyond the ultimate tensile threshold ($F_{ult}$), necking culminates in catastrophic fracture.
