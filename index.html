Physics & Constitutive Theory

1. Hooke's Law & Restoration

Within elastic bounds, the restoring force is linear to elongation $\Delta x = y - L_0$:

F_s = -k \cdot \Delta x

2. Equation of Motion (RK4 Solver)

Equating dynamic sum of forces including viscous damping and driving force:

m \cdot \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + c \cdot \frac{dx}{dt} + k \cdot x = F_0 \cos(2\pi f t)

3. Topologies

Parallel: $k_{eq} = k_1 + k_2$  |  Series: $\frac{1}{k_{eq}} = \frac{1}{k_1} + \frac{1}{k_2}$

4. Tensile Yield & Fatigue

Exceeding $F_{yield}$ causes plastic dislocation creep. Beyond $F_{ult}$, cohesive shear fractures the spring.
